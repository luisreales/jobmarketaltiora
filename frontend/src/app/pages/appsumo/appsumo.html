How it works

1. Navigates appsumo.com/software/ and extracts all categories.

2. Iterates every product page per category.

3. Filters reviews to 1, 2, and 3 taco ratings only.

4. Saves complaints to the database — deduplicated.

5. Scrape state is resumable. Re-running skips "Done" products.
